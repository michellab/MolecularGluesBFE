## Experimental data for CRBN- and DCAF16-based systems

- CRBN_exp.csv contains the K_d and corresponding $\Delta G_{bind}$ values from [1]
- DCAF16_exp.csv and DCAF16_abundances.csv contain the ITC $\Delta G_{bind}$ and normalised abundances values from [2]

[1] Miñarro-Lleonar, M.; Bertran-Mostazo, A.; Duro, J.; Barril, X.; Juárez-Jiménez, J. Journal of Medicinal Chemistry 2023, 66, 6037–6046
[2] Hsia, O. et al. Nature 2024, 627, 204–211.
